# Directions

1. **Night Court Glass**: a dusk pickleball court seen through smoked ink glass, with lime court lines, an orange ball as the only warm signal, and depth from layered panes.
2. **Cream Daylight Glass**: frosted cream glass over a sunlit court in paddlebase.org's light theme, with soft green shadows.
3. **Scoreboard Brutal**: hard Archivo scoreboard numerals, flat lime and ink blocks, split-flap transitions, and glass used only for the form.

## Killed

- **Cream Daylight Glass**: light glass on a cream background loses the blur and refraction that make glassmorphism read. It needs a white tint with dark text, and moving video behind it hurts contrast. It is also too close to paddlebase.org itself, so the event would not feel like its own moment.
- **Scoreboard Brutal**: it fights the brief. Flat blocks give up the glass and 3D parallax the client asked for, and the split-flap look reads as "sports bar", not as an invite for court owners.

## Kept: Night Court Glass

It uses the brand's real night mode (paddlebase.org ships ink `#0F1512` and `--color-night`) and its lime accent, which is at its strongest on ink (14.5:1). The court geometry under the glass ties the style to the audience, which is court owners and operators. They see their own product, the court, in every layer. Archivo is already the site's "scoreboard" font, so the date and court counts get a real brand voice instead of decoration.
